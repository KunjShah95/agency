**Short answer:** an AI agent is software that **understands a request in plain language, looks up the answer in your own information, and takes an action**. It might answer a customer, book a slot, or create an order. A small business needs one when it handles **the same questions or the same steps many times a day**, especially on WhatsApp.

## Chatbot vs AI agent

Old chatbots followed a fixed script: press 1 for timings, press 2 for prices. If a customer asked anything else, they got stuck.

An AI agent:

- understands questions however they're phrased, in English, Hindi or Gujarati
- answers from **your** documents: price list, policies, catalogue, FAQs
- can **do** things: check a sheet, create a booking, send a confirmation
- knows when to stop and **hand over to you**

## Real uses for a small business

- **Answering repeat questions on WhatsApp:** timings, location, prices, availability. Customers get an answer in seconds, even at 11 pm.
- **Taking orders or bookings:** collect the details, check availability, confirm, and log it in a sheet.
- **Qualifying leads:** ask the three questions you always ask, then pass only serious enquiries to you with a summary.
- **Internal help:** let your staff ask "what's our return policy for X?" instead of asking you.
- **Inside your product:** an assistant built into your app or website.

## When an AI agent is *not* worth it

Be honest about this before spending money:

- You get only a few enquiries a day. Just reply yourself.
- Every conversation is unique and needs your judgement.
- Your information isn't written down anywhere yet. Write it down first; that alone helps.

## How a good agent is built

1. **Pick one job.** "Answer pricing and availability on WhatsApp" beats "do customer service".
2. **Collect your real answers:** price list, policies, the questions you get every day.
3. **Build it on your information**, so it doesn't invent answers.
4. **Add guardrails:** it says "I'll connect you with the owner" whenever it isn't sure.
5. **Test it on real past questions** before customers ever see it.
6. **Review the conversations** in the first weeks and improve them.

## Getting started

Write down the five questions customers ask you most. If you answer them more than ten times a week, an agent will probably pay for itself.
