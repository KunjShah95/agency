**Short answer:** to be found on Google in Ahmedabad,

1. set up and verify a **Google Business Profile**,
2. collect **real reviews**,
3. have a **fast website** that clearly says what you do, where, and for how much,
4. keep your **name, phone and address identical everywhere** online.

The full checklist is below, in order of impact.

## 1. Create your Google Business Profile
Go to business.google.com. It's free. This is what puts you on Google Maps and in the local results with the map.
- Pick the most specific **category** (for example "Physiotherapist", not "Health").
- If customers don't visit you, set it up as a **service area business** covering Ahmedabad, and your home address stays hidden.
- Verify it. Unverified profiles rarely show up.

## 2. Fill in every field
Hours, phone, website, services with prices, and a description written the way customers talk. Add **real photos**: your shop, your work, your team. Profiles with complete details and photos look more trustworthy to both people and Google.

## 3. Ask every happy customer for a review
Reviews are one of the strongest local signals. Send customers the review link from your profile right after a good experience. **Reply to every review**, including the bad ones, politely.

## 4. Keep your name, phone and address identical everywhere
"Shah Dental Clinic, +91 98…" on Google, your website, Justdial, Facebook and Instagram should match **exactly**. Mismatches confuse Google about whether they're the same business.

## 5. Have a website that answers the obvious questions
Your website should say, on the first screen: **what you do, where, and how to contact you**. It also helps to show prices or "starting from" prices, your area, and a WhatsApp button. People and Google both reward clarity.

## 6. Make it fast on a phone
Most local searches happen on mobile, often on patchy data. A heavy site loses visitors before it loads. Test your site with Google's free PageSpeed Insights.

## 7. One page per service
"Root canal treatment in Satellite" and "teeth whitening in Satellite" are different searches. A page for each service, with its own title and a few FAQs, gives Google something specific to rank.

## 8. Answer questions in plain words (AEO)
Write FAQs exactly as customers ask them ("How much does X cost in Ahmedabad?") and answer in the first sentence. Google often quotes answers like these directly in search results.

## 9. Get listed where your customers look
Justdial, IndiaMART, industry directories, your association's website, and local news. Each consistent listing and link from another site builds trust.

## 10. Let AI assistants find you (GEO)
People now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini for recommendations. These assistants rely on what they can read about you on the web: your website, your listings, your reviews. Make sure your website states your services, area and prices clearly, and that AI crawlers are allowed to read it.

## Where to start this week
Do steps 1 to 4 first. They're free and make the biggest difference for local searches. Then improve your website.
