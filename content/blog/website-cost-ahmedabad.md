**Short answer:** a good small business website in Ahmedabad costs roughly **₹15,000 to ₹40,000**, a larger multi page site or web app **₹60,000 to ₹1.5 lakh**, and custom platforms more. At Srijan Studio a focused website starts at **₹15,000** and goes live in **7 days**, with the price fixed in writing before work starts.

The number on its own tells you very little, though. Two quotes of ₹20,000 can be completely different products. Here is what actually drives the price, so you can compare quotes properly.

## What you get at each price

### Around ₹15,000 to ₹40,000: a focused business website
Good for shops, clinics, advisors, consultants and studios that need to be found and contacted.

- 1 to 5 pages: home, services, about, contact
- Custom design for your brand, not a recycled template
- Mobile first, because most of your visitors are on a phone
- WhatsApp and call buttons
- Basic Google setup: titles, descriptions, a sitemap

### Around ₹60,000 to ₹1.5 lakh: a full launch build
For businesses that sell online or need more than a brochure.

- Multi page site or web app
- A design system so every page looks consistent
- Features like booking, a product catalogue or a client login
- Content structure planned for search from day one

### Above ₹1.5 lakh: custom platforms
Marketplaces, dashboards, AI features and integrations with other software. These are priced per project after a proper scope.

## What actually drives the cost

1. **Number of pages and how different they are.** Ten pages that share a layout cost far less than ten unique ones.
2. **Custom design or template.** A template is cheaper up front but looks like thousands of other sites.
3. **Features.** Booking, payments, multiple languages and logins each add real work.
4. **Content.** If you provide the text and photos, it costs less than having them written and shot.
5. **Speed and SEO.** A site built to load fast and rank takes more care than one that just looks right in a screenshot.

## Hidden costs to ask about before you pay

- **Domain renewal:** your `.com` or `.in` renews every year. Make sure it is registered in *your* name.
- **Hosting:** some setups are free (Srijan sites are hosted on Vercel), others charge monthly.
- **Page builder subscriptions:** Wix, Squarespace and similar builders charge every month, for as long as the site exists.
- **Changes after launch:** ask what a small text or photo change costs once the site is live.
- **Who owns the code:** if you can't take your website elsewhere, you are renting it, not buying it.

## How to compare two quotes

Ask every agency or freelancer the same five questions:

1. Is the price fixed, and is it in writing?
2. Exactly which pages and features are included?
3. When will it be live?
4. Who owns the domain, the code and the design files?
5. What are the yearly running costs?

If a quote can't answer these clearly, it isn't really a quote yet.
