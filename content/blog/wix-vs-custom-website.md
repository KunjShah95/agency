**Short answer:** use a website builder like Wix if you need something online today, for free or cheap, and Google traffic doesn't matter much yet. Choose a **custom built website** if you want to be found on Google, load fast on phones, avoid paying a subscription forever, and actually own your site.

Both are reasonable choices. They just suit different stages of a business.

## The quick comparison

| | Website builder (Wix, Squarespace) | Custom website |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Low | Higher, one time |
| Ongoing cost | Monthly subscription, forever | Domain plus low or free hosting |
| Speed on phones | Often heavy, lots of scripts | Built lean for your exact pages |
| Google ranking | Possible, but limited control | Full control over speed and structure |
| Design | Template based | Designed for your brand |
| Ownership | You rent it | You own the code |
| Leaving | Rebuild from scratch | Move it anywhere |

## When a website builder makes sense

- You are testing an idea and don't know if it will last.
- You have no budget yet, and something is better than nothing.
- You only need a single page with your phone number and address.

## When a custom website is worth it

- **Customers search for what you do.** "Physiotherapist in Satellite", "LIC agent in Ahmedabad", "jewellery wholesaler Manek Chowk". Ranking for these needs a fast, well structured site.
- **Most visitors are on phones**, often on patchy mobile data. Every extra second of loading loses people.
- **You want WhatsApp at the centre.** A custom site can open a WhatsApp chat with the enquiry already written out, so a visitor is one tap from messaging you.
- **You don't want a bill every month** for the rest of your business's life.
- **You want to own it.** If you ever change developers, a custom site goes with you.

## Speed matters more than most people think

Google uses page experience, including how fast a page loads and responds, as part of how it ranks results. It also matters to people: a slow site on mobile data loses visitors before they ever see your offer. Builders load a lot of shared code on every page. A custom site only ships what that page needs.

## The real cost over time

The fair comparison isn't "₹0 vs ₹15,000". It's *the subscription × the years you'll run the business* vs *a one time build plus a yearly domain*. Do that maths with today's builder prices before deciding. For most businesses that plan to be around in three years, the custom site comes out ahead, and it's yours.
